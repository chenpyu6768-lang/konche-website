PHILIPPINES SOLUTION

A boiler feed water system is the set of filters, softeners and purification equipment that prepares water before it enters your boiler. Its job is to stop the three things that quietly destroy boilers: scale, corrosion and carryover. What it consists of depends on three factors — your boiler's pressure, your raw water quality, and what the steam is used for.

WHAT IT PROTECTS

ProblemWhat actually happensWhat treatment does
Scale (hard water)Minerals coat the boiler tubes like rock; heat passes poorly, fuel use creeps up, tubes fail years early.Softening (and RO where needed) removes hardness before it reaches the boiler.
Corrosion (dissolved oxygen)Oxygen in the water eats into pipes and the boiler drum — rust, leaks, thinning metal.Deaeration removes dissolved gases; pH is kept in the safe band.
Carryover (high dissolved salts)Boiling water foams and droplets ride the steam into pipes and food-contact equipment.RO cuts dissolved salts so steam stays clean.

Actual results depend on your water, boiler and duty — KONCHE confirms every configuration after reviewing your boiler specification and water report.

Your situationTypical setupTypical plants

FAQ

Do I need just a water softener, or a softener with RO?

Lower-pressure boilers with reasonably good raw water often need only a softener; higher pressure, or hard / salty / silica-rich water, needs softening plus RO. The decision comes from your boiler's water specification and a raw water test — KONCHE reads both before proposing anything.

What should I prepare before asking for a quote?

Three things: boiler pressure and steam capacity (T/H), a raw water analysis (hardness, TDS, silica minimum), and how your steam is used. With these, you get a configuration and price range quickly.

Does KONCHE install in the Philippines?

KONCHE exports complete systems from 0.25 to 50 T/H with commissioning guidance and startup support, working with your local installer or boiler contractor — often through our OEM & partner channel.
