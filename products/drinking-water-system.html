What Is a Direct Drinking Water System?

KONCHE direct drinking water systems purify municipal tap water on site and distribute it through a circulating pipe network: GB 5749 output, ≥99.99% sterilization, ≥90% salt rejection, 0.5–50 m³/h.

What it delivers

  • Reduces dissolved salts, organics, particles and microorganisms
  • ≥99.99% sterilization performance under suitable conditions
  • Final water quality verified against GB 5749 or applicable local standards

How we scope every project

  • No two water sources are the same.
  • Each system is built from:
  • Source-water report (site-specific)
  • Served population and peak demand
  • Governing standard (GB 5749 or local equivalent)

The final configuration — including disinfection barriers, materials and performance guarantees — is confirmed after engineering review, not before.

FAQ

Can the system be connected directly to a drinking-water point?

Yes. The system can be configured with hygienic storage, terminal UF and point-of-use distribution. Final verification follows the local drinking-water standard.

Does RO remove microorganisms?

RO provides a strong barrier, while UV and terminal UF provide additional disinfection and particle protection.

Can the system replace bottled water?

For schools, communities, offices and factories, local purification can reduce bottled-water procurement and storage. Capacity and distribution should be designed around actual demand.

How is the capacity selected?

Use the number of users, peak demand, operating hours and storage volume. Konche normally selects capacity around peak demand with buffer storage.

Can the produced water be drunk directly?

Yes. KONCHE drinking water systems use a multi-stage RO + UV + ultrafiltration process. The output fully meets GB 5749, the Chinese hygienic standard for drinking water quality — bacteria, viruses, heavy metals and other parameters all comply — so the water is safe to drink directly.

What are the advantages over barreled or bottled water?

The system purifies and dispenses on site, removing the recontamination risks of bottled-water transport and storage. There are no buckets to carry or store, drinking-water costs drop to roughly 30–50% of barreled water, and supply is continuous.

Does the system need a dedicated operator?

No. Fully automatic control with intelligent monitoring handles operation, flushing and filter-change alerts automatically. Only periodic filter replacement and simple inspection are needed — school or community facility staff can manage it part-time.
