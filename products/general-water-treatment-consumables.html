PRODUCT DOCUMENTATION

General consumables for RO, UF, ultrapure-water and pretreatment systems: RO membrane elements, filter cartridges, filter media, ion-exchange resin and EDI modules. Stock supply, compatibility confirmation and replacement planning are available.

Complete technical consumables scope

The KONCHE general consumables programme is a five-series genuine-parts supply for RO, pretreatment, ion-exchange and EDI systems: RO membrane elements (4040/8040, selected by model; typical system desalination 97–99%), PP melt-blown cartridges (5–40 inch, 5/10/20 μm), pleated cartridges (10–40 inch, 0.1–1 μm), activated-carbon cartridges and media, quartz-sand media (0.5–1 mm / 1–2 mm), 001×7 softening resin, UP6250 polishing resin to 18.2 MΩ·cm, and EDI stack replacements with 3–10 year service life. All items fit KONCHE and mainstream-brand equipment, ship with factory test reports and support non-standard custom processing. Documented replacement cycles keep systems on specification: PP cartridges 3–6 months, pleated and carbon cartridges 6–12 months, media and resins by differential pressure and water report. Typical users: semiconductor and PCB plants, optics and optoelectronics lines, pharmaceutical GMP systems, food and beverage, municipal and school drinking-water projects, electroplating lines.

FAQ

How do I confirm a replacement filter?

Provide the equipment model, filter length, micron rating, material, connection and a product photo if available.

How often should RO membranes be replaced?

A common reference is 2–3 years, but feed-water quality, pretreatment and operating pressure determine actual life.

Can EDI replace mixed-bed resin?

EDI can provide continuous deionization without chemical regeneration in a suitable pretreatment and feed-water design.

Can Konche supply consumables for an existing system?

Yes. Compatibility is confirmed from the system model, part specification and operating requirement before quotation.
