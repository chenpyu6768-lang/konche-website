254 nm low-pressure high-intensity UV for chemical-free water disinfection. Typical capacity is 0.5–300 m³/h, sterilization performance can reach 99.99% in suitable conditions and lamp life is typically 8,000–10,000 operating hours.

KONCHE low-pressure sterilizers use 254 nm high-output quartz lamps in polished 304/316L chambers. Capacities span 0.5–300 m³/h with ≥99.99% inactivation and 8,000–10,000 h lamp life; in-line installation is same-day, and 185 nm versions handle ultrapure-water TOC duty. Typical projects: food plants, purified-water systems, secondary-supply tanks in buildings, pools and aquaculture.

FAQ

Can UV remove dissolved salts or particles?

No. UV provides disinfection. Filtration or RO is required for particles, dissolved salts and other impurities.

Is a fine filter required before UV?

A fine filter is recommended when particles or turbidity may reduce UV transmittance.

Does UV leave a residual disinfectant?

No. UV is chemical-free and does not leave a disinfectant residual, so downstream protection and hygiene should be considered.

How is the correct UV model selected?

Selection uses flow, UV transmittance, target dose, water temperature, pressure and installation conditions.
