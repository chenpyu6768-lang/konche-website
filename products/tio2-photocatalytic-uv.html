PRODUCT DOCUMENTATION

The UV photocatalytic titanium-dioxide (TiO₂) sterilizer combines medium-pressure UV with a TiO₂ photocatalytic baffled chamber. UV energy acting on the catalytic surface generates strongly oxidizing hydroxyl radicals (OH·) that crack refractory organic molecules into smaller, readily degradable compounds — or fully to CO₂ and H₂O.

TECHNICAL PRINCIPLE

In wastewater duty, molecules of the strong-oxidizing species absorb UV light on the TiO₂ catalytic surface and are excited to form hydroxyl radicals (OH·). The radicals react with hard-to-degrade pollutants, breaking them into small, easily degradable molecules — or fully into CO₂ and H₂O for harmless discharge.

FAQ

What is TiO₂ photocatalysis?

UV light acting on a titanium-dioxide catalytic surface generates hydroxyl radicals (OH·) — strongly oxidizing species that decompose refractory organic pollutants.

Is a TiO₂ photocatalytic system a standard product?

No. Each package is engineered to order against a verified water analysis and treatment target.
