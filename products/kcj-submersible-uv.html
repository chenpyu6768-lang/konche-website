PRODUCT DOCUMENTATION

KCJ submersible sterilizers work inside the storage vessel itself: the sealed lamp-and-sleeve assembly acts on both the stored water and the residual air in the tank headspace, maintaining tank cleanliness and preventing secondary contamination at the point of draw-off.

KCJ is the first choice for high-level tank outlets, storage-tank outlets and booster-pump inlets in compartmentalized or timed supply — used across electronics, pharmaceutical, food and cosmetics water systems. Selection follows the tank geometry, treatment flow and water quality; basic configuration is the UV lamp, high-transmission quartz sleeve, electronic ballast and connection fittings.

FAQ

In-tank or in-line UV — which do I need?

In-line units disinfect flowing water in the pipe; in-tank KCJ units disinfect the stored water and the tank headspace where secondary contamination occurs.

How is the model selected?

By tank geometry, draw-off flow and water quality — the assembly length and lamp power are matched to the vessel.
