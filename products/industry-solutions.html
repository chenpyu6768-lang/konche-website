An industry solution is a water treatment configuration selected for a defined application duty rather than a generic catalogue system. KONCHE configures solutions across food and beverage process water, drinking-water purification, general industrial pure water, seawater and brackish-water desalination, and electronics-grade ultrapure water. Each direction combines pretreatment, membrane separation — reverse osmosis with 97–99% typical salt rejection — polishing such as EDI to 18.2 MΩ·cm, and 254 nm or 185 nm UV disinfection. Typical equipment families include single-stage and double-stage RO trains, ultrafiltration pretreatment, UV sterilizers and ozone systems from 1 to 200 g/h. Containerized packaging is available for remote or fast-deployment sites. The configuration is set by the verified source-water report, the target product-water standard, the demand profile and site conditions; performance values are quoted as ranges until engineering review confirms the design basis for the project.

SOLUTION 01

Industrial Pure Water & Ultrapure Water Solution

Overview

KONCHE engineers integrated pure and ultrapure water systems around feed-water quality, production capacity and the required conductivity or resistivity. The treatment train can combine pretreatment, RO, EDI, polishing, UV and point-of-use control.

Application Scenarios

  • Electronics and semiconductor manufacturing
  • Photovoltaic and new-energy production
  • Biopharmaceutical and fine-chemical plants
  • Industrial laboratories and quality control

Main Pain Points

  • Dissolved ions, particles and organics reduce yield and process consistency.
  • Variable feed water makes strict specifications difficult to maintain.

SOLUTION 06

Industrial UF Pretreatment Solution

Overview

KONCHE uses ultrafiltration as a stable membrane barrier for industrial source water and reuse projects. UF removes suspended solids, fine particles and colloids before downstream RO, helping stabilize feed quality and reduce fouling pressure.

Application Scenarios

  • Factory raw-water pretreatment
  • Mining and process-water clarification
  • RO membrane pretreatment
  • Industrial water reuse front end

Main Pain Points

  • TSS, sediment and colloids foul downstream RO membranes.
  • Frequent cartridge replacement and cleaning increase operating cost.
  • Variable turbidity destabilizes conventional pretreatment.

Recommended Equipment

UF Ultrafiltration System↗RO Reverse Osmosis System↗UV Water Sterilizer↗

Treatment Outcomes

  • UF intercepts suspended solids and colloidal load.
  • More stable RO feed protects membranes and recovery performance.
  • Reduced fouling pressure lowers cleaning and consumable demand.
