PRODUCT DOCUMENTATION

KCF closed-vessel sterilizers disinfect water inside a polished 304/316L pressure reactor. Closed-vessel UV suits small-to-medium flows and any duty where pressure must be maintained and open channels are impractical; lamps can be mounted parallel or perpendicular to the flow — parallel layouts reduce head loss, perpendicular layouts raise turbulence and disinfection efficiency.

Four connection forms cover 2–600 m³/h: KCF-UV/A threaded monolithic, KCF-UV/B flanged split, KCF-UV/C split with automatic wiping, plus sight-glass built-in forms on request.

WORKING PRINCIPLE

Water enters the reactor and flows around the germicidal lamp held in a high-transmission quartz sleeve. 254 nm UV damages microbial DNA/RNA within seconds. The basic configuration: UV lamp, high-transmission quartz sleeve, electronic ballast, 304/316L stainless reactor, and a control box with hour-runner and alarm indication; sight port and manual or automatic wiping are optional.

FAQ

Which KCF form should I choose?

Small flows suit the threaded monolithic KCF-UV/A; larger or fouling-prone duties take the flanged KCF-UV/B or the auto-wiping KCF-UV/C.

Does UV change the water taste?

No. UV is chemical-free and leaves no residue, taste or color.
