TECHNICAL PORTFOLIO OVERVIEWRead the complete equipment and engineering scope

KONCHE is a water treatment equipment manufacturer that designs and builds project-engineered water treatment systems for overseas EPC contractors, engineering integrators and industrial clients. The portfolio covers reverse osmosis with 97–99% typical salt rejection in single-stage configurations, industrial and laboratory ultrapure water systems reaching 18.2 MΩ·cm, hollow-fiber ultrafiltration, 254 nm and 185 nm UV disinfection, and ozone systems sized from 1 to 200 g/h. Containerized plants and replacement consumables complete the offer. Since 1997, KONCHE has supported projects with process selection, equipment engineering, in-house manufacturing, factory acceptance testing, shipment documentation and technical guidance. Every system is engineered around a verified feed-water report and a defined product-water standard, not a generic capacity figure. Support covers equipment design, in-house manufacturing, factory acceptance testing and technical manuals and remote document clarification, with spare-parts supply for the installed base.

Frequent Questions

Short, factual answers about working with KONCHE. Product-level questions are answered on each system page.

What does KONCHE deliver?

What does KONCHE deliver?

KONCHE is a water treatment equipment manufacturer for industrial and commercial projects, based in China and operating since 1997. The portfolio covers reverse osmosis with 97–99% typical salt rejection, ultrapure water reaching 18.2 MΩ·cm, hollow-fiber ultrafiltration, 254 nm and 185 nm UV disinfection, and ozone systems sized from 1 to 200 g/h, alongside containerized plants and replacement consumables. Overseas EPC contractors, engineering integrators and project owners are supported from equipment design and manufacturing through factory acceptance testing and technical follow-up.

Scope

Equipment supply, spare-parts sourcing and complete packaged equipment delivery for projects worldwide.

Does KONCHE supply standard systems or customized systems?

Both. KONCHE supplies standardized equipment ranges such as single-stage RO systems and laboratory pure-water systems, and configures customized plants around a verified feed-water analysis, capacity target and applicable standard. Customization covers process selection, materials, instruments, controls and skid or container packaging.

What information does KONCHE need to quote a water treatment system?

A source-water analysis (or water-source description), required flow rate (e.g., m³/h), target product water quality, applicable destination standards (local environmental/discharge regulations), system recovery rate, daily operating hours, utility conditions (e.g., electricity, steam, compressed air, drainage), installation constraints (including footprint, headroom, load-bearing, access for maintenance), and local voltage/frequency. Please also specify the industry and final use of the treated water, as this affects the process selection.

Which regions and industries does KONCHE serve?

KONCHE supports overseas EPC contractors, engineering integrators and industrial clients worldwide. Our project portfolio spans across electronics, new energy, pharmaceuticals, food and beverage, direct drinking water, chemical processing, and seawater desalination.

Can KONCHE guarantee a specific product-water quality?

Performance values on this website are reference ranges for the stated conditions. A project guarantee is only set after engineering review of the feed-water analysis, demand profile, recovery target, operating environment and local utilities, and the proposal states the conditions under which each quoted value applies.

Does KONCHE support overseas EPC projects?

Yes. We provide equipment design, in-house manufacturing, and factory acceptance testing (FAT). For overseas projects, we supply detailed manuals, drawings, electrical information and other agreed technical documents, with remote clarification when required. KONCHE's supply ends at documented equipment delivery; destination setup is handled independently by the buyer. The proposal clearly defines supply boundaries, exclusions and responsibilities.
