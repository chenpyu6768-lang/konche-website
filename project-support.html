PROJECT SUPPORT SCOPE

Support matched to the equipment package.

The proposal defines the engineering inputs, documents, testing, exclusions and responsibilities for each project.

01

Process & equipment definition

Treatment stages and major equipment are configured from source-water analysis, capacity, target quality, recovery, operating hours and utilities.

02

Technical documents

Equipment layouts, process diagrams, electrical information, manuals and other project documents are confirmed in the agreed document list.

03

Factory integration & testing

Skids, piping, instruments and controls are assembled and checked before shipment according to the approved scope.

04

Delivery & document follow-up

Packaging and shipment documents are coordinated with manuals, drawings and remote technical clarification. KONCHE's supply ends at documented equipment delivery; destination setup is handled independently by the buyer.
