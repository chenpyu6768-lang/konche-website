How do I request a KONCHE quotation?

A KONCHE project inquiry is a written or telephone request for a water treatment proposal, sent to Konche.China3143@outlook.com. KONCHE reviews water treatment inquiries for EPC contractors, engineering integrators and industrial clients. An efficient technical review needs six inputs: the source-water analysis or water-source description, the required treatment capacity and daily operating hours, the target water quality or applicable project standard, the industry, process and final use of the treated water, the installation location, climate and available footprint, and the local voltage, frequency and automation requirements. A complete inquiry lets the engineering team propose the treatment stages, design basis, major equipment and the conditions attached to quoted water-quality values. Recovery or wastewater constraints, redundancy expectations and destination-market regulatory requirements complete the minimum data for an engineering inquiry. KONCHE, a manufacturer based in Bao'an District, Shenzhen, Guangdong, China and operating since 1997, handles these inquiries through its technical team.

What happens after an inquiry

Inquiries are routed to the technical team for an engineering review against the stated conditions. KONCHE then confirms suitable process directions, reference configurations and the information still missing before a binding proposal can be issued.
